Privacy Policy for Loan Affordability Calculator

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the Loan Affordability Calculator app ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles and why. The App is a calculator and estimation tool. It works without accounts and keeps the figures you enter on your own device.

1. Your financial inputs stay on your device

The income, expense, loan, asset and settings figures you enter are stored only locally on your device. We do not transmit them to our servers, we do not sell or share them, and we have no way to see them. You can delete your loan, income or expense data inside the App at any time, and removing the App removes this locally stored data.

2. Crash and diagnostic reporting (Sentry)

To find and fix problems, the App uses Sentry to send crash and diagnostic reports when something goes wrong. These reports can include technical details such as the type of error, a stack trace, the App version, and general device and operating-system information. They are used only to diagnose and improve the App's stability. They are not used to identify you and are not sold. The financial figures you enter are not the purpose of these reports.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing / App Store)

If you upgrade to the premium version, the purchase is processed by the app store on your platform (for example, Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store). The store, not us, handles your payment details. The App only receives confirmation of whether a valid purchase exists so it can unlock premium features.

4. No accounts and no advertising tracking

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. It does not display third-party advertising and does not use advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers.

5. PDF reports

If you export a PDF report, it is created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose. We do not receive a copy.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Not financial advice

The App provides estimates only. Please read the App's disclaimer and speak to a qualified adviser before you borrow.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the App and on this page. Please review it periodically.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please contact us at yoursimpleapps@gmail.com.
